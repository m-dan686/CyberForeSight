Attack: Port Scan

A port scan attempts to discover open ports and services on a network device.

Indicators:
- Connections to many different ports
- Repeated connection attempts
- Short connection durations
- Unusual destination-port patterns

Risk:
Port scanning can be reconnaissance before a more serious attack.

Recommended response:
- Monitor the source
- Identify the scanning device
- Alert the administrator
- Restrict the source if scanning is confirmed
